# Public comparison page

`/compare` lives on **oncobase.io** (`specs/oncobase-site.md`); on Diana's domain it redirects there. It helps people see how Oncobase compares with other tools, and nudges most of them toward something simpler. It is public, indexable, and shares the features page's look (`specs/features-page.md`): the same two-row header (`PublicChrome.tsx`: a primary row that is identical to the features page's, and a sub header with this page's sections), tables, accordions, and footer, plus `compare.css`.

## Purpose and tone

Most people don't need to build anything. The page says so first ("Not sure? Start with Notion to share with family today, or Obsidian to keep everything private on your own computer. If you want help researching your case, look at Yuga Bio."), discloses that Oncobase's authors wrote it ("we're not neutral"), and points to Oncobase only for the goals it fits: a private, shareable knowledge base you control, or building your own. Keep it that way. Do not rank products or add claims that make Oncobase look better than the facts support.

## Sections

1. **Hero** with the nudge and the disclosure, in the same textured band as the landing and features heroes.
2. **Which should I use?** Six goals (share notes with family, keep everything private, research and trial matches, collect records, a private knowledge base for a care team, building your own). Each names a product to start with, others to consider, why, and when to move to Oncobase. Four of six send people elsewhere, and a unit test keeps most goals pointed at something other than Oncobase.
3. **Side by side.** A table of eleven questions across Oncobase (first, so it stays in view next to the sticky question column), Notion, Obsidian, Yuga Bio, Citizen Health, Mere Medical, and Medplum. Cells carry a check, cross, or dash plus text. A dash means "Not stated": the product's own site or repository doesn't say, which is not the same as no.
4. **Each option.** One collapsed accordion per product (tagline always visible; "Great at" and "Keep in mind" inside).
5. **Building your own.** Two cards (family: have an agent set it up and read `/features.md`; company: fork, reuse, pair with a clinical data layer, and check which rules apply, since Oncobase carries no compliance certification), a table of the reusable Oncobase packages (only the CLI is on npm; the rest live under `packages/`), a table of other open-source projects worth building on (Medplum, Mere Medical, Cornerstone3D, OpenSeadragon), and "also worth knowing" (osteosarc.com, OnCo technologies, Healthier Intelligence, Fasten Health).
6. **Sources and corrections**, with an as-of date and a link to open an issue.

## Single source and agents

`src/pages/compare-data.ts` holds every product, cell, goal, package, and source. The page and `public/compare.md` (the markdown twin, generated by `bun scripts/build-llms-txt.ts`, linked from `llms.txt` and `features.md`) both render from it, and `compare-data.test.ts` fails on drift.

## Accuracy rules

Facts about other products come from their own sites and repositories, checked October 2026 (`COMPARE_ASOF`). Re-check a product before changing what the page says about it, and say "Not stated" rather than guess. Corrections made during the first pass, as examples of what to watch for:

- Fasten's on-premise repository (GPL-3.0) is archived, and its README says it supports manual entry and FHIR uploads, not syncing from providers. Mere Medical (MIT, active) is the self-hosted records tool the page features; Fasten appears only under "also worth knowing".
- Notion signs a HIPAA business associate agreement only on its Enterprise plan.
- Yuga Bio de-identifies uploads, searches 13 trial registries, is offered to US residents, and covers providers and custom plans with a business associate agreement; it does not train on user data without opt-in consent.
- Obsidian's license is proprietary; its file format is open markdown.
- Healthier Intelligence's site shows only a tagline and a contact address, so there is nothing to compare yet.

Claims about Oncobase follow the features page's accuracy rules.
