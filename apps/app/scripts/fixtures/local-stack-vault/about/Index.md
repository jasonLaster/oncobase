# About this wiki

This vault mirrors the shape of a real treatment wiki with a small number of
pages:

- **`wiki/`** synthesized notes: treatment plans, biomarkers, logistics.
- **`sources/`** raw materials: papers and reports.
- **`private/`** sensitive pages visible only to the care team.

## Conventions

Frontmatter `tags` are lowercase and hyphenated. Browse the [tag list](/tags)
to see what is already in use.
