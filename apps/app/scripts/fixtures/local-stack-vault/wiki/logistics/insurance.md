# Insurance

Insurance paperwork for infusion visits lives here. Keep a copy of the
[insurance card](/wiki/logistics/insurance-card.pdf) handy.

## Prior authorization

1. Oncology office submits the request.
2. Plan responds within 3 business days.
3. Appeals go through the patient advocate.
