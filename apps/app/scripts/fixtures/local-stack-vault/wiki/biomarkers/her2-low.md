# HER2-low

HER2-low is defined as IHC 1+ or IHC 2+ with a negative ISH result.

> [!note] Why it matters
> HER2-low tumors may be eligible for trastuzumab deruxtecan in the
> metastatic setting.

## Scoring

The fraction of stained cells is summarized as

$$
\text{score} = \frac{\text{stained cells}}{\text{total cells}} \times 100
$$
